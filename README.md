# Awesome-Interactive-Product-Demo-Platform

## Top Interactive Product Demo Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Interactive Product Tours, Demo Sandboxes, Sales Demos, Guided Walkthroughs & Buyer Enablement*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Interactive Product Demo** software. These systems let teams create clickable product tours, guided demos, and sandboxed experiences so prospects can explore software without a live sales engineer.



**Examples** include Navattic, Storylane, Walnut, Consensus, Reprise, Demostack, Saleo, Arcade, ScreenSpace, and TestBox (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full-featured interactive demo platforms are almost entirely commercial. Practical open options include **open demo builders**, self-hosted sandbox environments, interactive tutorial frameworks, and screen-capture-based tour tools. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Navattic](https://www.navattic.com/)**  

  Market-leading interactive product demo platform for HTML-based click-through tours used by product marketing and sales teams.



- **[Storylane](https://www.storylane.io/)**  

  Interactive demo and demo-center platform with freemium entry, AI-assisted features, and tools for marketing and sales demos.



- **[Walnut](https://www.walnut.io/)**  

  No-code interactive demo platform popular with enterprise product marketing for personalized, shareable product experiences.



- **[Consensus](https://goconsensus.com/)**  

  Demo automation platform focused on personalized video and interactive demos that scale sales engineering capacity.



- **[Reprise](https://www.reprise.com/)**  

  Enterprise demo and sandbox platform for creating realistic, isolated product environments for sales and presales.



- **[Demostack](https://www.demostack.com/)**  

  Demo environment platform that helps teams spin up and customize realistic product demos for sales cycles.



- **[Saleo](https://www.saleo.io/)**  

  Demo personalization platform that overlays custom data and messaging onto live product demos for sales teams.



- **[Arcade](https://www.arcade.software/)**  

  Interactive demo and walkthrough tool based on screen recording, popular for quick product tours and marketing content.



- **[ScreenSpace](https://www.screenspace.io/)**  

  Interactive product demo and storytelling platform aimed at engaging website visitors with guided product experiences.



- **[TestBox](https://www.testbox.com/)**  

  Demo and sandbox platform focused on providing isolated product environments for evaluation and sales.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Propels and open interactive demo builders](https://github.com/Propels-AI/Propels)**  

  Open-source tools aimed at recording and embedding interactive product demos on marketing sites to capture leads.



- **[Self-hosted sandbox / preview environment open tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Open platforms that spin up isolated app instances or preview URLs—usable as a foundation for product demo sandboxes.



- **[Interactive tutorial and product-tour open frameworks](https://github.com/)**  

  Libraries (e.g., intro.js-style guides, shepherd.js, and similar) for building in-app guided walkthroughs.



- **[Screen recording + annotation open stacks](https://github.com/)**  

  Open tools for capturing UI flows and adding hotspots or step guidance for simple product tours.



- **[Storybook and component-driven demo open setups](https://github.com/storybookjs/storybook)**  

  Open UI development environments often adapted to showcase product features in isolation for demos.



- **[Static site generators for demo marketing pages](https://github.com/)**  

  Open SSGs used to host marketing-driven interactive demos and walkthrough content.



- **[Browser automation open frameworks (Playwright, Cypress)](https://github.com/)**  

  Tools that can script and record product flows for demo content or automated guided experiences.



- **[Embeddable widget open kits for demos](https://github.com/)**  

  Lightweight open components for embedding clickable demos or guided tours on websites.



- **[Lead-capture form open integrations](https://github.com/)**  

  Open form and CRM connectors often paired with self-built demos to capture high-intent visitors.



- **[Docker / container-based demo environment templates](https://github.com/)**  

  Open templates for packaging product instances as disposable demo environments.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Building simple **HTML/CSS/JS product tours** with open guide libraries for marketing sites.

- Using **self-hosted sandboxes** for technical evaluation demos when full commercial platforms are overkill.

- Combining open recording tools with commercial demo platforms for hybrid workflows.

- Accepting that polished no-code editors, personalization at scale, analytics, CRM integrations, and enterprise sandbox isolation still favor commercial platforms (Navattic, Storylane, Walnut, Reprise, Demostack, Consensus, Arcade, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on cost control, data ownership, and lightweight marketing demos.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Record or script key product flows → add guidance hotspots → host on a marketing page or self-hosted sandbox → capture leads → hand off to sales. Suitable for startups and product teams with engineering support. Most B2B SaaS companies adopt commercial interactive demo platforms for speed and buyer experience.
